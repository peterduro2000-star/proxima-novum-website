Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Proxima Novum ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our applications (TailorPro, Tilemate, RoofMate, and Poultry Pro) collect, use, and safeguard your information.

1. Information Collection

Our apps are designed with an offline-first philosophy. Most data you enter into the apps—such as customer measurements, farm records, or construction estimates—is stored locally on your device.

  • Personal Information: We may collect your email address or phone number if you choose to create an account for cloud backup or sync features.
  • Usage Data: We may collect anonymous technical data to help us improve app performance and fix bugs.

2. How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and maintain our services.
  • Sync your data across your devices (if enabled).
  • Respond to your support requests.
  • Improve the functionality and user experience of our apps.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your business data is primarily stored on your own device. If you use our cloud features, your data is encrypted and stored securely. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect against unauthorized access.

4. Third-Party Services

Our apps may use third-party services for specific functions, such as:

  • Google Play Services: For app updates and licensing.
  • WhatsApp: When you choose to share reports or receipts via the WhatsApp integration.

5. Your Choices

You can use our apps entirely offline without creating an account. You have the right to access, update, or delete any personal information we hold about you by contacting us at our support email.
